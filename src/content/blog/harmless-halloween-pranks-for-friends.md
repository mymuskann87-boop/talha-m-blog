At a Halloween party two years ago, my friend Sara reached into a bowl of sweets and pulled out a small plastic spider sitting on top of the chocolates. She shrieked, then laughed so hard she had to sit down, then spent the rest of the night telling everyone about the spider. The spider went back into the bowl, and three more people fell for it before the night ended.

That is what a good Halloween prank looks like. Nobody is hurt, nothing is broken, and the person who gets tricked ends up laughing louder than everyone else. The line between a fun prank and a bad one is simple, but it matters a lot. Cross it, and a fun night turns into an upset friend. Stay on the right side of it, and everyone goes home with a story to tell.

## The golden rule first

Before you plan any prank, ask yourself one question: will the other person laugh within ten seconds? If the answer is yes, it is probably a good prank. If the answer is "maybe" or "probably not", skip it. A prank is a gift of laughter, not a test of someone's patience.

There are also firm limits. Never prank small children who might truly get frightened. Never damage anyone's clothes, phone, food, or home. Never lock someone in a room, hide something they need, or trick someone near stairs, roads, or water. And never use a prank to embarrass someone in front of a crowd. If a prank needs a victim, it is not a prank worth playing.

In my opinion, a prank only counts as funny if the other person laughs too. If only the prankster is laughing, that is not a joke. That is just being unkind with extra steps.

## Pranks that only make people laugh

These ideas are safe, simple, and easy to clean up:

**The candy bowl spider.** Place a plastic spider on top of the sweets in a bowl. People spot it, jump a little, and then laugh. Use a clearly fake spider, and never hide it inside food.

**The wobbly jelly cups.** Serve jelly in cups, but set a few jelly cups out that are actually made of orange jelly with a gummy worm on top. The surprise is the worm, and everyone can see it is a sweet.

**The switched labels.** Put funny labels on harmless things. A bottle of water becomes "Witch's Potion" and a bowl of popcorn becomes "Ghost Popcorn". Guests read the labels, smile, and help themselves.

**The talking pumpkin.** Hide a small speaker near a pumpkin and play a short recorded greeting when someone walks past: "Happy Halloween!" said in a cheerful voice. Keep it friendly, never scary or loud.

**The upside-down room trick.** In one corner of a party room, turn a few lightweight decorations upside down: a paper bat hanging the wrong way, a picture frame tilted. Guests notice slowly, one by one, and each discovery starts a new round of laughter.

**The mystery box.** Fill a box with peeled grapes and tell guests to feel inside and guess what it is. Most people guess "eyeballs" and then laugh when they see the grapes. Use clean food, clean hands, and a box that is easy to wash.

## Pranks to skip completely

Some pranks look funny in videos but are a bad idea in real life. Do not jump out at someone from behind a door, because a frightened person can fall or knock something over. Do not put fake blood on chairs or clothes, because stains can ruin things that matter to people. Do not swap someone's drink or food with something strange, because allergies and trust are serious things. And do not prank anyone who has already said they do not like pranks. Respect is part of the fun.

## If someone gets upset

Even a careful prank can land badly. Someone might be having a hard day, or the joke might touch a nerve you did not know about. If that happens, stop at once. Apologise simply and honestly, without excuses. Say you are sorry, mean it, and help clean up. Do not say, "It was only a joke," because that tells the upset person their feelings do not count. A quick, honest apology saves the friendship and often saves the evening too.

## The best prank of all

The pranks people remember are rarely the biggest or the loudest. They are the small, clever ones that make everyone laugh, including the person who fell for it. So keep your pranks light, keep them kind, and keep the plastic spiders far away from anyone's real food. Plan one good trick, enjoy the laughter, and then put the props away and enjoy the party.

Halloween is a night for friends, sweets, and silly fun. A harmless prank adds a spark to that night. A kind heart makes sure the spark never becomes a fire.

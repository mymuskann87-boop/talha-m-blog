**Scarlett Johansson** is one of the best-known actresses in the world. She started acting as a child, and today her films have earned billions of dollars. In this post I will tell her full life story in simple words, from her childhood in New York to her big movies, her awards, her family life, and her upcoming projects.

## Quick facts

- **Full name:** Scarlett Ingrid Johansson
- **Born:** November 22, 1984, in New York City, USA
- **Age:** 41 (as of October 2026)
- **Job:** Actress, singer, producer and director
- **Spouse:** Colin Jost (married in 2020)

## Early life and family

Scarlett was born and raised in New York City. Her father, Karsten Johansson, is a Danish architect. Her mother, Melanie Sloan, is a producer. She grew up in a creative home with several brothers and a sister, and she has a twin brother named Hunter.

From a very young age she wanted to be an actress. As a child she went to many auditions, and her mother supported her in this journey. She also studied at a professional children's school in New York, which helped her balance school and work.

## How her career began

Scarlett first appeared on stage as a child in an Off-Broadway play in New York. Soon after, she moved to films. Her first film was **North (1994)**, when she was still a child.

Her first big role came in **Manny & Lo (1996)**, where she played a young girl on the run. Critics liked her work, and she was nominated for an Independent Spirit Award at a very young age. In **The Horse Whisperer (1998)**, she acted next to Robert Redford, and the film made many people notice her talent.

## The breakthrough years (2003)

The year **2003** changed everything. She appeared in two famous films:

- **Lost in Translation:** She played a lonely young woman in Tokyo in this film by director Sofia Coppola. Her performance won her the **BAFTA Award for Best Actress**.
- **Girl with a Pearl Earring:** She played the girl in the famous painting by Johannes Vermeer. It was a very different role, and it showed that she could do serious period dramas too.

After this, she worked with respected directors in films like **Match Point (2005)**, **The Prestige (2006)** and **Vicky Cristina Barcelona (2008)**.

## Music and Broadway

Scarlett is not only an actress. She also released music albums, including **Anywhere I Lay My Head (2008)** and **Break Up (2009)**, and both of them reached the Billboard 200 chart.

In 2010 she made her Broadway debut in the play **A View from the Bridge**. Her stage performance won her a **Tony Award for Best Featured Actress**. This showed that she is strong both on the screen and in the theatre.

## Black Widow and the Marvel years

In 2010, Scarlett joined the Marvel Cinematic Universe as **Natasha Romanoff, also known as Black Widow**, starting with **Iron Man 2**. She played the character in many hit films, including **The Avengers (2012)** and its sequels. These films made her face known in every part of the world.

At the same time she kept making more unusual films:

- **Her (2013):** She gave her voice to an artificial intelligence called Samantha.
- **Under the Skin (2013):** A strange and quiet science fiction film.
- **Lucy (2014):** An action film about a woman who gains extraordinary powers.

Because of her success, she became the **world's highest-paid actress in 2018 and 2019**, and she has appeared many times on the Forbes Celebrity 100 list. Her films have earned more than **14 billion US dollars** worldwide, which puts her among the top box office stars of all time.

## Oscar nominations

In 2019, Scarlett received **two Oscar nominations in the same year**:

- **Best Actress** for **Marriage Story**
- **Best Supporting Actress** for **Jojo Rabbit**

She has also received several Golden Globe nominations during her career.

## Black Widow lawsuit

In 2021, the solo film **Black Widow** was released in cinemas and on streaming at the same time. Scarlett took legal action against Disney, saying the streaming release hurt her earnings. The case was settled later the same year. Many people in the film industry followed this story, because it was about how actors are paid when films come out online.

## Recent work

In recent years, Scarlett has kept her career busy in different directions.

- **Eleanor the Great (2025):** This was her first film as a director.
- **Jurassic World Rebirth (2025):** She played Zora Bennett in this big action film.
- **The Phoenician Scheme (2025):** She appeared in this film by director Wes Anderson.

## What is coming next

Reports say Scarlett has big projects ahead, including **The Exorcist**, a horror film directed by Mike Flanagan, and a role in **The Batman Part II**. Another film, **Paper Tiger**, is also listed for release. Release dates can change, so please check trusted sources for the latest news.

## Personal life

Scarlett has been married three times.

- **Ryan Reynolds:** They were married from 2008 to 2011.
- **Romain Dauriac:** She married him in 2014, and they have a daughter named Rose. They later separated.
- **Colin Jost:** She married the comedian and Saturday Night Live writer in 2020, and they have a son.

She usually keeps her family life private and does not talk about it much in public.

## Why people love her work

If you look at her career, you can see a pattern. She can play a quiet and lonely woman, a strong superhero, a voice without a body, and a mother in a painful divorce story, and every time she feels believable. Many fans also like that she moved between big blockbuster films and small, thoughtful films, instead of choosing only one type.

## Final words

Scarlett Johansson's story is a story of hard work. She started as a child actor in New York, and she became one of the most successful actresses of her time. With new films and her work as a director, her story is still going on.

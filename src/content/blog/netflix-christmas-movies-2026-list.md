Christmas movies are arriving early this year, at least in teaser form, and Netflix has now given us a first look.
Four festive films are on the way, arriving weekly between November and December, so there is something new to watch across the holiday season instead of everything landing on one day.

In this post I will explain what the first teaser showed, go through each film that was previewed, and share the release dates and casts that have been announced so far. I will also be clear about the films we only have titles for at this stage, because a teaser is a first look, not the full picture. Longer trailers are expected during the week, so some gaps should be filled in very soon.

## The short version

Netflix released the first teaser for its 2026 Christmas movie line-up through People magazine on October 11, 2026. The line-up has four festive films arriving weekly between November and December. "A Dog's Perfect Christmas" premieres on November 4, and "Tyler Perry's The Gospel of Christmas" arrives on November 11. The teaser also previewed two more films, "Guarding Stars" and "In a Holidaze". Longer trailers are expected during the week. That is the whole list in short. Now let us look at each film one by one, starting with the family story that opens the season.

## A Dog's Perfect Christmas opens the season on November 4

"A Dog's Perfect Christmas" premieres on November 4. It stars Dennis Quaid, Milo Ventimiglia, Jennifer Tilly and Mary Steenburgen.

The story is about a family held together by a teenager, her grandfather and his loyal basset hound. Even in that one short line, you can feel the shape of the film. It is a family story, it crosses generations, and a dog sits right at the heart of it. For many viewers, that is a very easy combination to warm to during the holidays.

The film is based on a novel by W. Bruce Cameron, the author of "A Dog's Purpose". That connection will mean a lot to readers and viewers who already know his name. If you enjoy gentle family stories where an animal helps hold people together, this sounds like the one to put first on your list. It also opens the whole line-up, arriving first on November 4, which gives it the job of setting the mood for the films that follow it each week.

## Tyler Perry's The Gospel of Christmas arrives on November 11

"Tyler Perry's The Gospel of Christmas" arrives on November 11, one week after the first film. It stars Fantasia Barrino and Tank.

The story follows a struggling pastor who inherits his family's church in a troubled New York neighbourhood. That is a heavier starting point than a typical festive film, and that is part of what makes it stand out in this list. It is a Christmas story, but it begins with responsibility, family history and a community facing hard times.

Music looks like a big part of this film too. Gospel legends Shirley Caesar, Fred Hammond, the Clark Sisters, Yolanda Adams and CeCe Winans appear in it. That is a long and impressive list of names for one film, and it tells you the gospel side of the story is not a small extra. It is built into the film itself.

## The other two films previewed in the teaser

The teaser also previewed "Guarding Stars" and "In a Holidaze".

That is, honestly, most of what we can say about these two films from the facts shared so far. Their titles were part of the first look, and they complete the group of four festive films in the 2026 line-up. But their stories, casts and exact release dates were not included in the details given with the teaser for this post, and I do not want to invent them for you.

## Why weekly releases can work well for families

Netflix said these four films will arrive weekly between November and December, and I think that rhythm suits Christmas movies very well.

It also makes the season feel longer, in a good way. Instead of one busy weekend of new films, you get a small event each week as December gets closer. The two films we know less about, "Guarding Stars" and "In a Holidaze", will fit into that same weekly pattern between November and December.

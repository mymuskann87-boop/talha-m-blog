Cristiano Ronaldo came back, scored, and let the football do the talking for a night.
On his return to club football for Al-Nassr, he scored the opening goal in a 3-0 win — and it was the 980th goal of his career.

If you missed the match, or you heard about the row with Portugal and want it explained simply, this is the full story in plain words.

## The short version

Ronaldo returned for Al-Nassr on Friday, October 9/10, after a controversial international break in which he walked out of the Portugal camp. He scored in the 23rd minute, putting in a rebound after Kingsley Coman's shot was saved. Samu Costa added a second in the 35th minute, and Coman scored in the 77th minute. The 3-0 Saudi Pro League win over Diriyah put Al-Nassr level on points behind leaders Al-Qadsiah on goal difference.

## How the 980th goal happened

The goal itself was simple, and that is often how the best strikers work.

In the 23rd minute, Kingsley Coman took a shot. The goalkeeper saved it. But a saved shot is not always a finished chance. The ball came back out, and Ronaldo was in the right place to put in the rebound.

A rebound goal may not look as pretty as a long shot into the top corner. But it tells you something important. You have to be alert, expect the ball might come back, and move before everyone else believes it will. This one was more than a normal opening goal, though. It was his 980th career goal, a total that already feels hard to believe.

## How the rest of the match went

Ronaldo's goal opened the scoring, but Al-Nassr did not stop there.

Samu Costa scored in the 35th minute to make it 2-0. That gave Al-Nassr a stronger hold on the match before half-time, and left Diriyah with an even harder job in the second half.

Coman then finished the scoring in the 77th minute. It was a neat full circle for him. His saved shot had led to Ronaldo's opening goal, and later he got a goal of his own to make it 3-0.

The result also mattered for the table. The win put Al-Nassr level on points behind leaders Al-Qadsiah on goal difference. In simple words, Al-Nassr have the same number of points, but Al-Qadsiah stay ahead because their goal difference — the gap between goals scored and goals allowed — is better.

Al-Nassr's coach is Ange Postecoglou, and a 3-0 win on a night full of noise off the pitch will please any coach.

## What did the fans do?

The supporters made their feelings clear, and they did it early.

ESPN reported that Al-Nassr fans showed a banner reading "greatness is earned" and chanted his name in the 7th minute.

A banner and a chant may sound like small things. In football, they are not. They tell a player, in front of everyone, that the home crowd is with him — and after a difficult, public row during the international break, that support was part of the story of the night.

## What was the Portugal row about?

To understand why this return mattered so much, you need to know what happened before it.

Ronaldo walked out of the Portugal camp during a controversial international break. As reported in this story, he was an unused substitute in Portugal's 2-1 Nations League win in Norway on September 27, and he said coach Jorge Jesus broke his word about playing him.

An unused substitute is a player named in the squad who does not come onto the pitch at all. For a player who expected to play, and who believes a promise was made, that can become a much bigger issue. For balance, he has said he remains available for Portugal, so this was not a player closing the door on his national team. His record explains the attention: he has 146 goals in 234 appearances for Portugal, numbers given in the reporting of this story.

## Why this goal mattered beyond one match

One goal does not settle a row with a national team coach. But it does change the mood. Before this match, the talk was about a walkout, a broken word, and a player left on the bench. After it, the talk could also be about a goal, a win, and a team level on points at the top. A player cannot control every headline — he can only give people a new one. On Friday, Ronaldo did that in the 23rd minute.

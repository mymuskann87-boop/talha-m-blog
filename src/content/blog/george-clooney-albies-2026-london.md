## What the Albies are really about

The Albies honour people who defend justice at great personal risk.

Please read that line again, slowly, because it is the key to the whole event. These are not awards for fame, and they are not awards for popularity. They recognise people and groups who stand up for justice even when doing so puts them at risk. That risk is part of the definition given for the Albies, and it should not be skipped over.

The awards were launched in 2022 and are named for Justice Albie Sachs, the South African judge and anti-apartheid campaigner. The idea behind the night is simple and smart: famous faces bring the cameras, and the cameras bring attention and protection to people whose work is dangerous. The Clooney Foundation for Justice supports legal aid and advocacy work in more than 40 countries, and the Albies are its biggest night of the year.

## Meet the 2026 honorees

The 2026 honorees are a mix of individuals and one organisation, from different parts of the world and different kinds of work.

They include Kyrgyz journalists Bolot Temirov and Makhabat Tazhibek Kyzy. Journalism is part of this list in a direct way, through their names and their work as journalists. The honorees also include Vanessa Mwangala, a girls' rights advocate from Malawi, who is recognised here for her advocacy work. American photojournalist Lynsey Addario is also among the honorees. A photojournalist tells stories through photographs, often from places and situations that many of us only ever see from far away.

The list also includes Justice Plus, a Congolese human rights organisation, showing that the Albies can honour a group as well as a single person. And it includes Brad Smith, who is a vice chair and president of Microsoft. His inclusion shows the list reaches beyond journalism and advocacy into other fields as well.

## Amal Clooney's gold Gucci gown stole the red carpet

The most talked-about look of the night belonged to the host herself. Amal Clooney arrived in a gold Gucci gown covered in sparkling sequins and swinging beaded fringe, finished with a crystal clutch and gold heels. Photographers could not get enough of it, and the dress was across entertainment news within hours.

The choice of Gucci was not random. Gucci was the event's exclusive fashion partner this year, alongside sponsors including Microsoft and Charlotte Tilbury Beauty. George Clooney kept his own look classic: a black tuxedo and bow tie, walking the carpet hand in hand with Amal.

## George Clooney's emotional moment

Behind the glamour, the night had a personal and emotional side. At the event, George Clooney broke his silence about the death of Presley Gerber, the son of his best friend Rande Gerber, who passed away last month.

It was a reminder that even on a night built for cameras, real life is never far away. Clooney's friendship with the Gerber family goes back decades, and his words about their loss were one of the most quietly powerful moments of the evening — a famous man using a famous night to stand beside a grieving friend.

## Before Sunrise, the Queue Was Already There

It was still dark when the first people arrived. They came with folding chairs, flasks of tea, and the kind of patience you only see when something genuinely new is about to happen. Nobody queues before dawn for an ordinary phone. But the phone waiting behind those glass doors was not ordinary at all. It bends. It opens like a book. And for the very first time in Apple's history, it folds.

Today, 23 October 2026, is the day the iPhone Duo officially went on sale in India and the other first markets around the world. Apple announced the phone on 9 September 2026, and pre-orders opened on 16 October. Those early orders kept the lucky ones busy all week. But this morning belonged to everyone else — the walk-in crowd, the curious, the ones who simply wanted to stand in a shop, hold the thing in both hands, and fold it for themselves.

So come along. Let us walk through this first day of sales together, from the sleepy queue at dawn to the moment the first buyer walked out smiling, phone in pocket, history in hand.

## The Moment the Doors Opened

There is a small ceremony to every big Apple launch, and it never really changes: the staff gather inside, the shutters rise, and the crowd claps as the first customers walk in. What changed today was the sound that followed. It was not just applause. It was a soft wave of *oohs*, repeated again and again, table after table, as demo units were picked up and opened.

The trick is simple to describe and strangely magical to feel. Closed, the iPhone Duo looks like a normal phone, with a 5.4-inch outer display on the front for quick jobs — checking a message, glancing at a map, skipping a song. Then you open it, and the inside blooms into a wide 7.6-inch display, closer to a small tablet than a phone. One moment it fits in your palm. The next moment it fills both hands like an open book.

People in the queue had watched videos of this all week. Still, watching a video and doing it yourself are two very different things. Fingers kept opening and closing the phone, slowly, then faster, like children with a new toy they cannot quite believe is real.

## Why a Folding Phone Feels So Different

A foldable phone is not just a phone with a hinge. It changes what a phone is *for*.

Apple designed the iPhone Duo for three big things: multitasking, gaming, and watching content on that larger unfolded screen. Think about what that means in an ordinary day. You can read an article on one side while your notes sit on the other. You can reply to a message without closing the video you were watching. A game gets room to breathe. A film stops feeling squeezed.

In my view, this is the first iPhone in years that makes people stop and stare in a shop again — not because it is faster, but because it *moves* differently from every phone they have owned before.

Under the surface, there is serious engineering working quietly. The phone runs on Apple's A20 Pro chip, the company's powerful new processor. Foldable phones generate heat in tight spaces, so Apple built a custom vapour chamber for cooling, a thin chamber that spreads heat away from the busy parts. There is also a dual-battery design, with battery power split to suit the folding shape instead of relying on one single block. And because this is a modern Apple device, it supports Apple Intelligence and Siri AI, so the smart assistant features come along for the ride on both screens.

None of that technical talk filled the shop this morning, of course. Shoppers rarely ask about vapour chambers. They ask two questions: "Can I open it?" and "Which colour should I get?"

## Star White or Night Sky?

The iPhone Duo comes in two colours: Star White and Night Sky. All morning, the demo tables turned into small debates. Star White looks clean and bright, the kind of colour that shows off the fold with a soft shine. Night Sky is darker and moodier, with a deep finish that hides fingerprints better and looks serious in the hand.

There is no wrong answer, and the staff knew it. By mid-morning, the friendly argument had become part of the entertainment for people still waiting their turn.

## A Short Walk Back: How We Got Here

It helps to remember that this day was a long time coming. Apple officially announced the iPhone Duo on 9 September 2026, confirming that its first foldable smartphone would launch in October. Pre-orders began on 16 October, giving the most eager fans a head start. Then came today, 23 October, when general availability began and the shops opened their doors to everyone.

People have whispered about a folding iPhone for years. Every year, the rumour returned, and every year, the phone stayed flat. That long wait is exactly why this morning's queue felt less like a shopping trip and more like the end of a very long story. The folding iPhone is no longer a rumour, a drawing, or a guess. It is a product. It has a name, two colours, two screens, and a price of admission that begins with standing in line and ends with that first satisfying fold.

## What the First Buyers Did With It

The fun of a launch day is watching what people do in the first five minutes of ownership.

One buyer opened the phone flat on the table and just stared at the big screen, the way you stare at a new view from a window. Another went straight to a game, thumbs flying, grinning at the extra space. A third — and this might be the most honest review of all — spent a full minute opening and closing the fold, slowly, without opening a single app. Sometimes the feature *is* the fold.

This is also the moment where the 5.4-inch outer display earns its keep. Most people do not want to unfold a phone just to read one short message. So the Duo lives two lives: a quick, one-handed phone on the outside, and a wide, comfortable screen on the inside, ready when you are. Closed for the small jobs. Open for the good stuff.

## One More Thing People Are Whispering About

Launch week always brings extra gossip, so let us handle this part carefully. Alongside the confirmed news, reports suggest that Apple may have more hardware coming this month, possibly including a touchscreen MacBook Pro, an OLED iPad mini, and a refresh of the iMac line with an M6 chip. These are **unconfirmed reports**, not announcements — Apple has not confirmed them, and they may change or never appear at all.

What *is* confirmed is already plenty: the iPhone Duo is real, it is on sale from today, and it is the headline act of Apple's October.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the iPhone Duo?

The iPhone Duo is Apple's first foldable smartphone. It has a 5.4-inch outer display for quick tasks and opens up to reveal a larger 7.6-inch inner display for multitasking, gaming, and watching videos.

### When did iPhone Duo sales begin?

Apple announced the iPhone Duo on 9 September 2026. Pre-orders began on 16 October 2026, and general availability started on 23 October 2026 in India and other initial markets.

### Which chip powers the iPhone Duo?

The phone is powered by Apple's A20 Pro chip. It also uses a custom vapour chamber for cooling and a dual-battery design, and it supports Apple Intelligence and Siri AI.

### What colours does the iPhone Duo come in?

It comes in two colours: Star White and Night Sky.

### Is a touchscreen MacBook Pro also launching this month?

That is only an unconfirmed report. Reports suggest a touchscreen MacBook Pro, an OLED iPad mini, and an M6 iMac refresh could arrive this month, but Apple has not confirmed any of them.

## As the Sun Went Down on Day One

By evening, the queue was gone, the demo tables were quieter, and the first wave of owners had carried their folding phones out into the city — onto trains, into cafés, and onto sofas where family members would all ask the same question: "Show me the fold again."

Launch days come and go. Phones get faster every year, and most of those days blur together and are forgotten. But some days mark a genuine turn, the day a company finally does the thing everyone said it would never do. Apple spent years as the company that would not fold. Today, in shops across India and the other first markets, it folded — beautifully, in Star White and Night Sky, one happy customer at a time.

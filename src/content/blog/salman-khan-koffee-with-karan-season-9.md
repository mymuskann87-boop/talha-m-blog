Every famous couch has a story, but very few couches become famous themselves. The Koffee With Karan couch is one of them. For more than two decades, India's biggest film stars have sat on it, laughed on it, cried on it, fought on it, and sometimes said things on it that became headlines for weeks. Now, that couch is being used one final time. Koffee With Karan is returning for its ninth season — and it will be the last season ever. The farewell run begins on October 14, 2026, streaming on JioHotstar, and the makers have chosen the perfect person to sit down first: Salman Khan.

For fans, this is not just another season premiere. It feels like the closing of a long chapter of Indian entertainment. A show that started in 2004, when many of today's young viewers were not even born, is finally saying goodbye. And it is saying goodbye in style — with "the one & only bhaii," as host Karan Johar himself wrote while announcing the news.

## The Announcement That Made Fans Emotional

The end of the show was not a sudden decision dropped on the audience overnight. On September 30, filmmaker and host Karan Johar announced that the ninth season would be the final one, sharing an emotional message looking back at how the show began and what it had become. He spoke honestly about the journey — the friendships, the memories, the madness, the love, and also the controversies, trolling and gossip that followed the show everywhere it went.

Johar made it clear that he knew exactly what was coming. There would be trolls, there would be comments, there would be posts — and he was ready for all of it. But beneath all that noise, he said, there was still abundant love for this cup of coffee. It was the message of a man closing a door with gratitude instead of regret.

Then, on October 12, the excitement jumped to another level. Johar shared a promo video on Instagram confirming that Salman Khan would be the very first guest of the final season. His caption said it all in a few words: "One last time... a koffee with the one & only bhaii." In the promo, Salman appears in his trademark relaxed style and confirms his return himself, telling viewers in Hindi that yes, he is coming to do Koffee With Karan. Within hours, fans were flooding social media with reactions — excitement, nostalgia, and a little sadness that this ride is ending.

## Why Salman Khan Was the Only Choice to Open the Farewell

Karan Johar could have picked any superstar in India to open the final season. He picked Salman Khan, and long-time viewers understand exactly why. Salman is not just a guest on this show; he is part of its history. He opened Season 4, delivering one of the most talked-about episodes the show ever produced. He then returned for a milestone moment — the 100th episode in Season 5 — which he shared with his brothers, Arbaaz Khan and Sohail Khan. That family episode remains one of the most loved in the show's entire run, full of childhood stories and the kind of honest, unfiltered conversation that made the show famous.

There is also something symbolic about it. Koffee With Karan built its name on getting guarded superstars to drop their guard. Salman Khan, with his dry humour and his habit of saying exactly what he thinks, has always been the guest who never needed much pushing. Starting the final season with him tells the audience what kind of season this will be: big, candid, emotional, and a little unpredictable. As Johar joked in the promo, this season could go anywhere — and Salman, smiling, seemed to agree.

It has also been a long wait. Salman has not sat on that couch since Season 5 in 2016, nearly ten years ago. A whole generation of viewers has grown up hearing about his famous Koffee moments without ever watching one live. Now they will get their chance, and so will the fans who were there from the beginning.

## 22 Years, 8 Seasons and 161 Episodes of Pure Pop Culture

To understand why this farewell matters, you have to understand how big this show has been. Koffee With Karan premiered in 2004 on Star World, with Shah Rukh Khan and Kajol as its very first guests. At the time, the idea was simple and risky: a famous filmmaker sits down with famous friends and talks the way people talk when the cameras are supposedly not judging. It worked beyond anyone's imagination.

Across eight seasons and 161 episodes, the couch hosted almost every major name in Indian cinema — Amitabh Bachchan, Shah Rukh Khan, Aamir Khan, Deepika Padukone, Priyanka Chopra and many more. The show became a machine for creating moments: playful rivalries, romance confessions, shocking revelations, and playful games that fans quoted for years. Episodes regularly spilled out of television and into national conversation. Headlines were born there. Memes were born there. Some friendships were even tested there.

The show also changed with time. Its sixth season ended on television in 2019, after which episodes lived on streaming, reaching a younger, global audience. Through every change, one thing stayed the same: when a Koffee episode dropped, everybody talked about it the next day. Very few talk shows anywhere in the world manage to hold that kind of cultural space for 22 years.

## Why Karan Johar Is Ending It Now

So why stop a show that still draws this much attention? According to Johar, the answer is about where he wants to go next. In an interview with Variety, he explained that he wants to dedicate the next phase of his career to directing films and telling stories through cinema. It was, in his words, a heavy-hearted decision — not a show being cancelled, but a creator choosing his own ending.

That distinction matters to fans. Koffee With Karan is not fading away quietly because people stopped caring. It is ending because its creator feels the journey is complete — eight seasons, 161 episodes, and a final ninth season planned as a proper farewell rather than a slow decline. Johar has said some journeys are not planned to end; you simply know, deep inside, when it is time to let them go. This, he believes, is that time.

## What the Final Season Promises

The ninth and final season streams on JioHotstar's Peacock Hub from October 14, 2026, with Salman Khan in the hot seat for episode one. Beyond that, the makers have kept most of the guest list a mystery, which is exactly how this show likes it — rumours, speculation, and surprises have always been part of its fuel. Johar has already swatted away at least one rumour with his trademark sarcasm, making clear that cricketers are not on the guest list this season.

What viewers can expect is a farewell run designed to go out on a high: the biggest names, the most personal conversations, and a host who knows this is his last lap and intends to enjoy every minute of it. If the final season delivers even a few moments like the ones that built its legend, it will be remembered as one of television's great goodbyes.
